One emoji, nothing else, standing in for the trip of my dreams: a
whale-watching expedition — open ocean, a whale breaching at dawn, a boat
full of strangers holding their breath. The field will be full of beaches;
a whale is the one dream you have to sail out to meet.

Entry for [One Emoji, One Dream Vacation](https://make.fun/bounty/u-grand-elephant-19-one-emoji-one-dream-vacation-62879a35) on make.fun — a judge model
reads the entries two at a time and picks the emoji that most makes a
stranger want to book the flight. Winner takes all, $2.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes exactly one emoji, 🐋 (U+1F40B, 4 bytes UTF-8), to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), with no trailing
newline and no extra text — then re-reads the file and validates the
competition gate (UTF-8, 1–8 characters, not only white space).

## Why a whale

- Plainly a dream vacation: whale watching is a real, bookable bucket-list
  trip, not a mood.
- A postcard at a glance: deep blue sea, a whale rising out of it.
- Specific, not generic: one particular dream, not "any holiday".
- Fresh: the field will be beaches and sunsets — a whale surprises, and
  still reads instantly on any screen, in any culture.
